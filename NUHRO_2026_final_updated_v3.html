Introduction

MSOC Europe NUHRO 2026 is an annual Christmas carol singing competition that brings together parishes from across Europe to celebrate the birth of our Lord Jesus Christ through music, faith, creativity, and fellowship.

More than a competition, NUHRO is a celebration of unity, joy, and shared faith, providing an opportunity for parish communities to discover their talents, strengthen teamwork, and glorify God together through song and creative expression.

Eligibility & entry

Participants

  • All participants in the team must be approved by the respective Vicar.

Team size

  • Minimum 6 individuals, any age group.
  • Both songs must feature the same singers. If any of the original singers are unable to appear in the second song due to illness or other valid reasons, that is acceptable — but no additional singers are allowed.
  • Performance participants: for the short film, stage play, or other performance elements, teams may involve any members of their parish, provided they are approved by the Vicar.
  • These additional performance participants must not sing.

A true parish team effort

Parishes are strongly encouraged to make NUHRO 2026 a true parish team effort by involving their own church members in every aspect of the submission — singing, music, camera work, recording, editing, technical support, and all other behind-the-scenes activities.

We warmly encourage each parish to use the talents, creativity, and skills within its own church community, giving members an opportunity to participate not only in the performance but also in the entire production process.

Songs to submit

Each team must submit two songs. Song 1 is the same hymn for every team.

REQUIRED SONG — SONG 1

Shishuvayi Swayame Velivayi

ശിശുവായ്‌ സ്വയമേ വെളിവായി

From the Christmas service.

Listen to the reference recording (opens in a new tab)

Vocal performance guidelines

  • The reference song is provided with instrumental music only, as a guide. Participating teams must perform the song using vocals only — no instrumental music, background track, or karaoke track.
  • The tune and melody should follow the provided reference version as closely as possible. The song must be performed as a group, not as an individual solo performance.
  • A lyrics sheet may not be used during the performance.
  • Teams may arrange the vocals creatively — splitting parts between male and female voices, using different vocal sections, or singing selected portions together as a full group — while maintaining the original tune and character of the reference song. However, dividing the song into formal choral sections such as soprano, alto, tenor, and bass is not permitted.
4 minmaximum · vocals only

Video recording & submission guidelines

  • The singing must be recorded as a raw video using a mobile phone in landscape mode. If a camera is used, the same raw-recording requirements apply.
  • No video or audio editing, cropping, processing, filters, or enhancements are permitted.
  • External microphones, audio interfaces, mixers, or other external sound equipment must not be used.
  • Teams must submit the original raw, unedited video file directly from the recording device.
Risk of disqualification

Any form of audio or video editing or enhancement to Song 1 may result in disqualification.

Verification note: the organisers may verify the original recording file and device information/metadata to confirm the authenticity of the submitted video.

Song 2 — free-choice Christmas carol

  • Each team must perform one Christmas carol of their choice.
  • The selected song must be sung in Malayalam.
  • Karaoke tracks or live musical instruments are permitted — a maximum of 4 live musical instruments may be used.
  • A lyrics sheet may be used during the performance if required.
  • The video and singing must be recorded simultaneously as one genuine live performance. Separately recorded vocals, lip-syncing, dubbing, or replacing/adding the vocal track afterwards are not permitted.
  • The singing video must not be recorded in a studio. It should be recorded on a suitable stage or in a church hall, community hall, or similar performance venue.
  • Microphones may be connected directly to the camera, or through a normal amplifier or mixer setup. A standard audio mixer may be used for normal live sound balancing.
  • Vocal enhancement or artificial voice-processing tools are not permitted — including AI-based vocal enhancement, pitch correction, voice replacement, or any other technology that artificially alters or improves the singers' voices.
  • The submitted recording must represent the genuine live singing and musical performance of the team.
Recommendation — song selection (optional)

Teams are encouraged to consider selecting a new, less commonly heard, or rarely performed Malayalam Christmas carol to bring freshness and variety to the competition.

Please note: This is only a recommendation and is not a competition rule or judging requirement.

4–9 minmax

Duration:

  • Singing: Minimum 4 minutes.
  • Total performance: Maximum 9 minutes, including singing, acting and any other elements.

Christmas performance & creative presentation

PERFORMANCE THEME

“Christ Is Born — Let Peace Begin With Us”

The performance should creatively connect the birth of Christ with the message of peace, hope, love, and unity in today's world.

Performance guidelines

  • All members of the parish may participate in the performance.
  • Teams may present their performance as a short film/video production or a stage-style act, presented before or after the Free-Choice Christmas song.
  • Short-film or acting segments may be edited before or after the song, but no separate pre-recorded content may be inserted during the live singing section.
  • The complete presentation — Free-Choice song plus all performance elements — must not exceed 9 minutes.
No church-identifying information

Church/parish names, logos, banners, watermarks, or any other identifying references must not appear in the submitted video. Judges will receive only the Team/Chest Number, to ensure fair and impartial evaluation.

What judges look for

  • Relevance to the theme.
  • Connection between Christ's birth and today's world.
  • Clarity and impact of the message.
  • Creativity and originality.
  • Acting, costumes and visual presentation.
  • Overall coordination and team participation.

Video guidelines — Song 2

  • Original visuals: all visuals must be original and created by the team. Third-party footage and AI-generated video/images are not permitted.
  • Video quality: the final video must be submitted in high quality.
  • Spiritual standards: all visuals, costumes, actions, and presentation elements must be appropriate and maintain Christian and spiritual standards.
  • Live performance: during the song, the singers must be clearly and continuously shown performing live, with natural facial expressions, smiles, energy, and engagement appropriate to the song.
  • No inserted content during the song: once the singing section begins, short-film scenes, pre-recorded acting, external visuals, or other separate content must not be inserted between sections of the song.
  • Stage setup: teams are free to design their own stage setup, background, decorations, and positioning, provided all other competition rules are followed.
  • Multi-camera recording: multiple cameras and gimbals may be used, and teams may switch angles during editing, provided the footage shows the actual live performance and complies with all video guidelines.

Event day

  • The MSOC Europe NUHRO 2026 Event Day will be held online, in the esteemed presence of His Eminence Dr. Kuriakose Mor Theophilose Metropolitan, Patriarchal Vicar of Europe.
  • The programme will include a Christmas message from His Eminence, highlights from the competition, and the announcement of the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place winners.
  • Following the competition, all participating teams will receive the judges' feedback and their marks by email — valuable guidance for future participation and improvement.

While every team is encouraged to aim for the best result, NUHRO is about much more than winning. The most important purpose is to participate, discover and develop the talents within your parish, strengthen teamwork, and build greater unity among parish members.

Participate together, grow together, and celebrate Christmas together.

We wish all participating teams the very best for MSOC Europe NUHRO 2026!

Video submission

  • Upload all required video files to Google Drive, OneDrive, or a similar cloud storage service, and place them together in one folder named with your Church/Parish name.
  • Make sure all files are shared in a downloadable format, with the necessary permission to access and download them.
  • Please ensure the link and download permissions are working correctly before sending the email.

The Team Captain must email the folder link to:
carolmsoceurope@gmail.com

Files required for submission

  1. Song 1: original raw video file, exactly as recorded on the device, without any editing or enhancement.
  2. Song 2: final edited video file, prepared according to the Song 2 video guidelines.
  3. Group photo: one group photo of all participating team members.
  4. 1-minute verification clip: a behind-the-scenes video showing the recording process of the Free-Choice Song. Used by organisers/judges only, for verification and cross-checking.

Terms & conditions

  • All rules and guidelines stated above must be followed by every participating team.
  • The competition should be approached in a spirit of fellowship, unity, and celebration, rather than rivalry.
  • Failure to follow the rules may result in the submitted content not being published and/or not being considered for judging.
  • The MSOC Europe NUHRO Team reserves the right to reject or disapprove any submission that does not comply with the competition rules and guidelines.
  • The judges' decision is final.
